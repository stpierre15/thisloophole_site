THE LOOPHOLE STANDARD

A clear call.
A reason behind it.

We match your purchase to retailer policies, compare the economics we can support, and tell you exactly what to do next.

What goes into a verdict?

BUY, WAIT, SWITCH, NEGOTIATE, or DON’T BUY. Simple rules consider a comparable price, relevant benefits, and the information still missing. When we haven’t established the price is good, we say so.

What does the score mean?

Price, optionality, benefits, and timing contribute up to 25 points each. The score describes your current purchase, not product quality. Without comparison or eligible discount evidence, the score is withheld; it is not a probability or a guarantee. Every result shows the breakdown.

90–100 Excellent · 75–89 Good · 60–74 Fair · 40–59 Optimize or wait · 0–39 Poor.

How do you verify opportunities?

Each policy links to its primary source and shows when we checked it. Policies can change and your eligibility may need confirmation. We don’t claim live inventory, guaranteed discounts, or market-wide price coverage. We never add incompatible discounts or count warranties as cash savings.
