# Dealership catalog and image workflow

`epa-catalog.json` has 251 model/powertrain records across 35 brands drawn from the US EPA 2026 fuel-economy data download: https://www.fueleconomy.gov/feg/download.shtml. EPA rating is evidence of a rated configuration; it is not proof of current dealer inventory or retail availability. EPA-only records intentionally retain `null` in unverified fields and have `dataStatus: "EPA-rated; retail and dimensions unverified"`. They cannot be recommended. The 35-brand research roster includes every make named in the project brief plus Fiat; roster inclusion and recommendation eligibility are deliberately separate.

A record becomes `verified-eligible` only after checking a current manufacturer's US MSRP, seating, dimensions and useful specs, generating an anonymous 3/4 structural concept with `scripts/dealership/render-prompt.mjs <id>`, visually checking it for accidental brand or production styling, and adding an actual manufacturer exterior photo behind the reveal-only server endpoint. Keep anonymous filenames opaque. Generate prompts from the record so physical dimensions and packaging stay in sync; all renders share one studio/camera treatment. Any material data change requires checking the concept again. `catalog.mjs` is a server-side JavaScript export of the JSON data; regenerate it after editing the JSON.

Current production-eligible source pages: Chevrolet [Trax](https://www.chevrolet.com/suvs/trax); GMC [Terrain](https://www.gmc.com/suvs/previous-year/terrain); Ford [Maverick XL](https://www.ford.com/trucks/maverick/models/xl/); Dodge [Durango GT](https://www.dodge.com/durango/specs.gt.html); Honda [CR-V](https://automobiles.honda.com/2026/cr-v/specs-features-trim-comparison), [Civic sedan](https://automobiles.honda.com/civic-sedan/specs-features-trim-comparison), [Pilot](https://automobiles.honda.com/pilot/specs-features-trim-comparison); Subaru [Forester trim comparison](https://www.subaru.com/services/vehicles/pdf/trimComparison/2026/FOR); Toyota [RAV4](https://www.toyota.com/rav4/features/); Hyundai [IONIQ 5](https://www.hyundaiusa.com/us/en/vehicles/ioniq-5). Manufacturer or authorized dealer exterior images are served only after a user locks a blind selection. The four new reveal-photo sources are the [Chevrolet Trax trim selector](https://www.chevrolet.com/suvs/trax), [GMC Terrain product page](https://www.gmc.com/suvs/terrain), [Germain Ford Maverick XL page](https://www.germainfordofbeavercreek.com/new-2026-ford-maverick-xl/), and [Dodge Durango configurator](https://www.dodge.com/bmo.durango.html).

As of 2026-09-23, 13 of 251 records are eligible across 8 brands, with 10 distinct anonymous concept images and 10 manufacturer or authorized dealer exteriors. Do not use the remaining 238 entries as recommendations until verified, rendered, and photographed. Do not replace missing zero-to-sixty, reliability, resale, or safety values with estimates.
