# Dealership catalog and image workflow

`epa-catalog.json` has 253 model/powertrain records across 35 brands drawn from the US EPA 2026 fuel-economy data download: https://www.fueleconomy.gov/feg/download.shtml. EPA rating is evidence of a rated configuration; it is not proof of current dealer inventory or retail availability. EPA-only records intentionally retain `null` in unverified fields and have `dataStatus: "EPA-rated; retail and dimensions unverified"`. They cannot be recommended. The 35-brand research roster includes every make named in the project brief plus Fiat; roster inclusion and recommendation eligibility are deliberately separate.

A record becomes `verified-eligible` only after checking a current manufacturer's US MSRP, seating, dimensions and useful specs, generating an anonymous 3/4 structural concept with `scripts/dealership/render-prompt.mjs <id>`, visually checking it for accidental brand or production styling, and adding an actual manufacturer or authorized dealer exterior photo behind the reveal-only server endpoint. Keep anonymous filenames opaque. Generate prompts from the record so physical dimensions and packaging stay in sync; all renders share one studio/camera treatment. Any material data change requires checking the concept again. `catalog.mjs` is a server-side JavaScript export of the JSON data; regenerate it after editing the JSON.

`epa-reference.json` pins the EPA source row for every catalog entry. The catalog audit compares year, make, drivetrain, fuel, powertrain, combined efficiency, and range against those rows. EPA `rangeA` is the electric-only range of a plug-in hybrid; EPA `range` is its total driving range. Four Honda entries previously cited the wrong EPA drivetrain/engine rows, and 17 plug-in hybrids previously put total range in `evRange`; both are corrected and now checked automatically.
