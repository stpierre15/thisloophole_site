# Dealership catalog and image workflow

`epa-catalog.json` has 421 model/powertrain records across 45 normalized make names. The baseline is all 1,172 configurations in the US EPA 2026 fuel-economy download, grouped into 400 model/powertrain records: https://www.fueleconomy.gov/feg/download.shtml. `epa-model-roster.json` pins the complete coverage list. `scripts/dealership/refresh-epa-coverage.py /path/to/vehicles.csv` rebuilds the roster and imports newly rated models. It expands EPA's overbroad base-model names where they hide marketed vehicles, including Yukon XL, Blazer EV, Land Rover Defender sizes, BMW M models, and Golf R. EPA lists both `MINI` and `Mini`; the catalog uses `Mini`. The other 21 records are 2026 models listed by their manufacturers but absent from the EPA download, such as Toyota GR86, GMC HUMMER EV, and heavy-duty pickups and vans; their official source pages are pinned in `manufacturer-model-roster.json`.

An EPA rating is evidence of a rated configuration, not proof of current dealer inventory or retail availability. EPA-only records intentionally retain `null` in unverified fields and have `dataStatus: "EPA-rated; retail and dimensions unverified"`. Manufacturer-only records have `dataStatus: "manufacturer-listed; configuration unverified"`. Neither group can be recommended until its configuration and images are verified. This catalog covers the pinned source rosters; it is not a claim that every automaker's future or unrated offering exists in the 2026 EPA file.

A record becomes `verified-eligible` only after checking a current manufacturer's US MSRP, seating, dimensions and useful specs, generating an anonymous 3/4 structural concept with `scripts/dealership/render-prompt.mjs <id>`, visually checking it for accidental brand or production styling, and adding an actual manufacturer or authorized dealer exterior photo behind the reveal-only server endpoint. Keep anonymous filenames opaque. Generate prompts from the record so physical dimensions and packaging stay in sync; all renders share one studio/camera treatment. Any material data change requires checking the concept again. `catalog.mjs` is a server-side JavaScript export of the JSON data; regenerate it after editing the JSON.

`epa-reference.json` pins the EPA source row for every EPA-backed catalog entry. The catalog audit compares year, make, drivetrain, fuel, powertrain, combined efficiency, and range against those rows, then requires every EPA roster group and manufacturer-only model to have exactly one catalog record. EPA `rangeA` is the electric-only range of a plug-in hybrid; EPA `range` is its total driving range. Four Honda entries previously cited the wrong EPA drivetrain/engine rows, and 17 plug-in hybrids previously put total range in `evRange`; both are corrected and now checked automatically.

As of 2026-09-24, 15 of 421 records are eligible across 8 brands, with 12 distinct anonymous concept images and 12 manufacturer or authorized dealer exteriors. Do not use the remaining 406 entries as recommendations until verified, rendered, and photographed. Do not replace missing zero-to-sixty, reliability, resale, or safety values with estimates.

The dealership homepage is a needs-first filter form. Seats, cargo minimum (15/25/35 cu ft), base drivetrain or AWD availability, powertrain, budget, body style, towing, footprint, and sort order can be set without taking the quiz. The filter endpoint previews the count and creates a blind selection session; it returns all distinct verified model families that meet the hard constraints, with no quiz-style fit percentage. Cargo uses the published volume for the listed configuration, which may mean space behind an occupied third row or a pickup bed. The optional quiz stays in the top-right navigation and returns up to 10 distinct model families. Both routes use the same one-time blind choice and reveal.

Run `npm run check:dealership` to audit every catalog row, verify that all eligible models can enter a result set, and list makes that have only research-only records. Currently 406 records across 37 makes lack the verified model data and images needed for blind recommendations; they are not silently presented as selectable.
