# Dealership catalog and image workflow

`epa-catalog.json` has 250 model/powertrain records across 34 brands drawn from the US EPA 2026 fuel-economy data download: https://www.fueleconomy.gov/feg/download.shtml. EPA rating is evidence of a rated configuration; it is not proof of current dealer inventory or retail availability. EPA-only records intentionally retain `null` in unverified fields and have `dataStatus: "EPA-rated; retail and dimensions unverified"`. They cannot be recommended.

A record becomes `verified-eligible` only after checking a current manufacturer's US MSRP, seating, dimensions and useful specs, generating an anonymous 3/4 structural concept with `scripts/dealership/render-prompt.mjs <id>`, visually checking it for accidental brand or production styling, and adding an actual manufacturer exterior photo behind the reveal-only server endpoint. Keep anonymous filenames opaque. Generate prompts from the record so physical dimensions and packaging stay in sync; all renders share one studio/camera treatment. Any material data change requires checking the concept again. `catalog.mjs` is a server-side JavaScript export of the JSON data; regenerate it after editing the JSON.

Current production-eligible source pages: Honda [CR-V](https://automobiles.honda.com/2026/cr-v/specs-features-trim-comparison), [Civic sedan](https://automobiles.honda.com/civic-sedan/specs-features-trim-comparison), [Pilot](https://automobiles.honda.com/pilot/specs-features-trim-comparison); Subaru [Forester trim comparison](https://www.subaru.com/services/vehicles/pdf/trimComparison/2026/FOR); Toyota [RAV4](https://www.toyota.com/rav4/features/); Hyundai [IONIQ 5](https://www.hyundaiusa.com/us/en/vehicles/ioniq-5). Manufacturer exterior images are served only after a user locks a blind selection.

As of 2026-09-23, 9 of 250 records are eligible, with 6 distinct anonymous concept images and 6 manufacturer or authorized distributor exteriors. Do not use the remaining 241 entries as recommendations until verified, rendered, and photographed. Do not replace missing zero-to-sixty, reliability, resale, or safety values with estimates.
