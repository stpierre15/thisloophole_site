PRIVACY · SEPTEMBER 2026

Your purchase data.

Loophole works without an account. When you submit a check, we store the product description or link, price, merchant, purchase preferences, and the recommendation. Please leave personal information out of the description.

Why we store it

We use purchase checks and optional feedback to understand which recommendations help people and to improve our matching rules. If you report savings, we label them as self-reported until evidence is reviewed. You don’t need to submit feedback to use the service.

Browser identification

We keep a random identifier in your browser’s local storage to estimate repeat use. It does not contain your name or email. Clearing this site’s browser data removes that identifier. Your purchase result and its feedback-access token are held in this page’s memory and disappear on reload.

Service providers and links

Netlify hosts the site and stores the purchase records. Hosting systems process technical request information, including IP addresses, for delivery and abuse protection. Pasting a supported public retailer URL triggers a request from our server to that retailer to read product details. Advertising and session query parameters are removed first. We cache public listing details and source references to avoid repeated lookups. Public product links and detected product descriptions may be sent to Anthropic for cited retailer search. We do not send your payment preferences, feedback or account information for this search. When connected, Rainforest API receives the Amazon product identifier to retrieve public offer data. Lookup caches are reused for up to ten minutes; stored purchase results retain their source references. Retailer links open their websites, where their own privacy policies apply.

Your choices

Merchant and preference fields are optional. Purchase outcomes and savings amounts are optional. No bank, card-account number, receipt, or login is needed. We do not sell your purchase data or use affiliate links. Records are retained to measure outcomes; there is no account-based self-service deletion feature in this version.
