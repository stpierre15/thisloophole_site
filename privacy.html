PRIVACY · SEPTEMBER 2026

Your data should not become another pricing error.

LOOPHOLE works without an account. We store only what is needed to run experiments, improve matching, prevent abuse, and remember optional feedback.

Studio and The Same Thing

When you submit a product URL, we store the canonical public URL, extracted public product information, search evidence, the comparison, and technical timestamps. Public product data may be sent to Anthropic for bounded, cited retailer search. Repeated URLs use a cache so we do not rerun paid analysis unnecessarily. Demo comparisons use fictional data and make no external request.

If you request a better match or monthly updates, we store your email, the submitted URL when provided, the requested experiment, and a timestamp. There is no account. We do not sell this information. The form currently records interest; it does not enroll you in a third-party mailing platform.

We record basic first-party experiment events such as page views, submissions, successful comparisons, outbound clicks, sharing, and email submissions. Event metadata is restricted and does not include your email or full product URL.

Purchase checker

The purchase checker stores the product description or link, price, merchant, preferences, recommendation, and optional feedback. A random browser identifier estimates repeat use. Public retailer links may be read directly, searched through Anthropic, or—in the case of Amazon—looked up through Rainforest API. Source references are retained with results.

Infrastructure and choices

Netlify hosts the site and stores records in Netlify Blobs. Hosting systems process technical request data such as IP addresses for delivery and abuse protection. API keys stay on the server. No card number, retailer login, receipt, or LOOPHOLE account is required. Retailer links open third-party sites with their own policies.

Records are retained for product improvement and small-scale analytics. This MVP has no account-based deletion screen. To request deletion of an email capture, reply through the same contact channel that directed you to this site and identify the submitted address.
