PRIVACY / SEPTEMBER 2026

Your data.

The current site works without an account. The price leaderboard uses dated public retailer product records. Viewing the board does not send your product links or personal information to an AI service.

First-party events

We record basic experiment events: page views, opening a comparison, following a store link, and sharing. Restricted event metadata contains product identifiers and action names, not email addresses or full product URLs. Netlify hosts the site, stores these events, and processes technical request information for delivery and abuse protection.

Store links and sharing

Retailer links open third-party sites with their own privacy policies. We do not use affiliate links. Sharing uses your device’s share menu or copies text; we do not send messages on your behalf.

Earlier experiments

The purchase checker, live URL analysis, and email forms are currently paused. Previously submitted checks, feedback, public source records, and optional email-interest captures remain stored for product improvement. They are not sold. This MVP has no account-based deletion screen. To request deletion, contact us through the same channel that directed you to this site and identify the submitted address or record.
