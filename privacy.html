Your data.

The site works without an account. The store-price board uses dated public retailer records. The second-life tool uses the official eBay API when connected. Clearly labeled examples contain fictional prices and no purchase links.

Product searches

We cache product names, normalized attributes, canonical public product URLs and comparison results to avoid repeating requests. A supplied US ZIP is used for shipping estimates and is omitted from shared results. Product searches go to eBay when connected; Amazon product identification may use our licensed Rainforest connection. The circular search does not call an AI service.

Shared comparisons

Anyone with a result link can see its product, baseline price, listing comparison and sources. Email-alert records are stored separately and never included. Do not enter private information into product fields. Listings are dated observations; shared inventory can change or disappear.

First-party events

We record basic actions: page views, searches, provider outcomes, opening a comparison, following a listing, sharing and requesting an alert. Event metadata uses action names, categories and record identifiers, not email, ZIP or full product queries and URLs. Netlify hosts the site, stores records and processes technical request information for delivery and abuse protection.

Price-alert requests

If you submit your email, we save it with the product identity, optional desired price and timestamps. Requests are currently pending: automatic monitoring and email delivery are not connected. Your email is private, is not sold and does not create an account.

Store links and sharing

Store links open third-party sites with their own privacy policies. Live marketplace offers are rechecked before redirecting. We do not currently use affiliate links. Sharing uses your device’s share menu or copies text; we do not send messages on your behalf.

Earlier experiments and deletion

The purchase checker and earlier AI comparison endpoints remain paused. Previous checks, feedback and optional interest records remain private for product improvement. This MVP has no account-based deletion screen. To request deletion, contact us through the same channel that directed you to this site and identify the submitted address or result link.
